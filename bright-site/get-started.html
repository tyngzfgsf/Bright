Save it here

Paste the key below. It's stored under your account only — nobody else can read it.

Your key is stored in a database record tied to your Google account, protected by rules that only let you read or write your own record — not other users, not anonymous requests. It's used by pasting it into Bright's Settings screen on your phone; the site itself never makes API calls on your behalf.

Bright is built and maintained by one person, kept as free as possible on purpose.
