Features

Built for drilling, not browsing

Answer fast, under pressure, and find out right away whether you were right.

Scenarios, not scripts

Cardiac arrest, anaphylaxis, stroke and more — revealed one question at a time, in character. Or type your own case.

Scored as you go

Every answer gets a 0–10 score and specific feedback on what you missed — not just "good job."

Hands-free voice mode

Speak your answers and hear the case play out, with live captions and no button between turns.

Spaced review

Questions you miss come back right when you're about to forget them, one tap from the home screen.

Exam-aligned triage

Cases carry KTAS or ESI levels, with a focus on Korean national-exam style emergency training.

Korean and English

Follows your phone's language on first launch. Switch anytime — the AI answers in whichever you pick.

Skill profile

Session 50 is smarter than session 1.

Bright tracks your average score for every kind of emergency, spots your weakest area, and puts a one-tap drill for it on your home screen. A plain chatbot can roleplay a case — it can't remember where you struggle.
